07Battery, health and safety

Lastamp reads your iPhone’s battery level and charging state, and pays more cores when you start a run on a lower charge. It does not control charging or change any settings on your phone. Nothing in the game requires a low battery.

Your phone matters more than a run:

  • keep enough charge for calls, navigation and emergencies;
  • plug in whenever you need to: the run goes on, it just pays fewer cores;
  • stop if your phone gets hot, and use safe, certified chargers;
  • take regular breaks.

The game uses bright, flashing and fast-moving effects. If you have epilepsy or have ever had a seizure, talk to a doctor before playing. Stop at once if you feel dizzy, sick or unwell.
